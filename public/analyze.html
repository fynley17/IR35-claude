Reality check — 10 questions

Answer based on how you'll actually work, not what the contract says.

01 —Right of Substitution: If you couldn't work, could you send a qualified substitute in your place?
02 —Has the right of substitution ever been tested or used in practice?
03 —Control: Who decides how you carry out the work day-to-day?
04 —Equipment: Will you use your own tools and equipment to do the work?
05 —Multiple clients: Do you work for other clients at the same time?
06 —Termination: Can the client end the contract at very short notice without financial penalty?
07 —Integration: Will you be embedded in the client's organisation (team meetings, org chart, Slack/Teams, manager)?
08 —Financial risk: Do you bear genuine financial risk? (e.g. fixed-price deliverables, correcting errors at your own cost, professional indemnity insurance)
09 —Autonomy: Do you control your own working hours and location?
10 —Mutuality of Obligation: Does the contract guarantee work, or must you accept all work offered?
