Which should you choose?

  • Before you sign a new contract: an instant report tells you which clauses to push back on while you still have leverage.
  • Your client is small (so you decide your status): a documented review is part of showing reasonable care — consider a specialist review for high-value engagements.
  • You've received an inside-IR35 SDS you disagree with: use a report to structure your challenge.
  • You're under HMRC enquiry: go straight to a specialist adviser.

Is it tax-deductible?

A contract review bought by your limited company for its business is normally an allowable expense. Keep the receipt, and check with your accountant.
